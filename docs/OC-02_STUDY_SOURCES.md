## Field and relationship audit

**TIPNR.** One entity can occupy a block of lines with different shapes. The header includes the unified name and Strong identifier, a two-slot parent field, siblings, partners, offspring, tribe/nation, description and type. Additional lines carry unique and alternate names, Hebrew/Greek forms, disambiguated identifiers, occurrence references and ambiguity notes. The file also contains documentation and example headers, so counting lines or all section markers as people would overstate coverage. Preserve blank parent slots, collective names and distinctions between namesakes. A source-inferred connector must remain labelled as such. Name variants referring to modern translations are lexical metadata. No tagged translation corpus was acquired.

**Theographic people and relationships.** Preserve the Airtable record ID, source `personID`, names, aliases, ambiguity, family arrays, person-level reference lists and provenance. The audit projected **8,926 directed source assertions** from mother (200), father (1,584), children (1,785), partners (209), siblings (4,660), maternal half-siblings (4) and paternal half-siblings (484). All reference target IDs in the people table. Source direction and original field names survive. Each projected row has a source-record pointer and an unreviewed confidence label. Edge-specific passage and lineage-account fields remain null. A reciprocal entry is another source assertion. The processed projection has its own provenance receipt and CC BY-SA attribution.

**Theographic places and events.** Preserve competing OpenBible, Pleiades, Recogito and upstream coordinate fields, precision, aliases, ambiguity and source IDs. Preserve participants, locations, predecessor, range flag, duration, lag and date provenance on events. Old Testament/Gospels dates in this dataset follow Jones, and Acts dates draw on Atwood. Keep those as source chronologies. Upstream dictionary, comments and notes require their own attribution treatment before display. Verse text, word indexes and map assets were excluded. [Chronology notes](https://github.com/robertrouse/theographic-bible-metadata/blob/95c381da341187d072a9e8a0070536a474e2e11c/docs/chronology-and-dates.md)

**OpenBible identifications.** Keep ancient IDs apart from modern candidate IDs, references, candidate class, modifiers/resolutions, vote counts, time-weighted scores, source IDs, types and geometry credits. **748 of 1,342** ancient-place records have multiple candidate identifications. Missing or disputed identifications remain open. Do not turn an editorial vote score into a measured probability or resolve a place to a coordinate by highest score alone. A source record identifies a cited work and its contributors. Access to its bibliography grants no rights over the underlying book or image.

## Boundaries for the next data task

Keep Matthew 1 and Luke 3 as separate passage accounts. A source-level person reference does not establish a particular kinship edge. Parent, ancestor, legal descent, dynasty and succession require evidence for their own type. Relationships that disagree stay in separate source records. Future reconciliation needs passage-level citations, confidence, alternatives and reviewer decisions. No rows were merged or exposed through an API. [Study manifests](../data/manifests/study)
